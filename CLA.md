## Signing

Karst uses [CLA Assistant](https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant). The
first time you open a pull request, a bot will comment with a link. Follow it,
read this agreement, and click to sign. Your signature is recorded against
your GitHub account and covers all future pull requests — you sign once.
