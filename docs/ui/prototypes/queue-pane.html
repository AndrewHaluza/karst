300 px sidebar. Queue sits between Planning and Tickets, about 30% of the height while open, and scrolls on its own.

  1. Queue pane (new, between Planning and Tickets). One list of all pending drafts and not-started tickets, from every session. Collapsed by default.
  2. Position 1, 2, 3 … is the computed order: dependencies first, then priority, then age.
  3. Priority chip: URG HIGH LOW. No chip for normal, so most rows stay quiet.
  4. Id chip with session: D452 · P17 for a draft, T997 for a ticket.
  5. Why, on the right: ↑ D498 = priority inherited from a draft that waits on this one. waits D452 = an open blocker comes first.
  6. Click a draft row → Review form. Click a ticket row → its dashboard.
  7. Planning pane (D502): the draft card gets the priority chip. (D501): a cross-session dependency shows as needs D452 · P17.
  8. Tickets pane: a not-started ticket shows its priority chip too.
