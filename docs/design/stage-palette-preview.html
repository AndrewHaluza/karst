What the chip replaces

→
proposed impl done ship uat

Two ramps, one row. Left is unchanged — karst mark plus the status dot (in progress, needs you, blocked, shipped). Right is the stage chip on the stage palette. The chip stops paraphrasing status ("Implementing", "Shipped", "UAT failed" all encoded status in a stage slot, in two colors) and states the stage only — status is already the dot two columns left. Chip shape is unchanged: 10px, 9px radius, fill is color-mix(currentColor, transparent).

Open questions

  • The reference artifact would not open for me — claude.ai served it as a public reader and refused. Paste its hex values and I swap this table; nothing else in the design changes.
  • No stage may reuse a status color. impl was VS Code's chart blue — the same blue as "In progress" — so a row read blue twice for two different reasons. It is now indigo. The stage palette is checked against the status ramp (blue / amber / red / green) as a rule, not case by case; fix/red and done/green are the two where the meanings genuinely coincide.
  • Status ramp untouched — In progress (blue), Needs you (amber), Blocked (red) keep their colors and their job: the facet row and the dot on the mark. Only the wording changes: Shipped → Done, in the facet and everywhere else that phrase appears.
  • Status phrases leaving the chip: today the chip says "Implementing", "Shipped", "UAT failed", "Needs you" — status wearing a stage slot. The proposal makes it the stage key alone. Status still reads from the dot on the left, and the full phrase stays in the row tooltip.
  • A ticket with no stage yet ("Not started") gets the dashed unknown chip reading none — tell me if you would rather it show no chip at all.
  • done is green — completion reads green everywhere else in the product. ship takes magenta, the one hue not already spoken for by another stage or by the running/blocked status colors.
