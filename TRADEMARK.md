## What you may do without asking

- Refer to Karst by name in articles, talks, documentation, tutorials,

- State truthfully that your software works with, integrates with, supports,
  or is built on Karst.
- Use the name in the title of a book, video, or post *about* Karst.

  and notices intact.
- Use the logo to link to the project or to illustrate a reference to it,

This is ordinary nominative fair use. You do not need permission and you do
not need to ask.

## Forks and modified versions

You may fork Karst under the terms of the `LICENSE`. When you distribute a
modified version, please do not distribute it under the name "Karst".

Give your fork its own name. You may say, accurately and prominently, that it
is "based on Karst" or "a fork of Karst" — that is encouraged, and no
permission is needed for it. What is not permitted is presenting your fork as
though it were Karst itself, because that is what leaves users unable to tell
whose software they are running.

This is the only real restriction here, and it exists so that "Karst" keeps
meaning one specific thing.

## Requests

For anything requiring permission, or if you are unsure whether your use is
covered, open a discussion at
https://github.com/AndrewHaluza/karst/discussions and tag it `trademark`.

Reasonable requests are generally granted. Asking is cheap, and it is better
than guessing.
