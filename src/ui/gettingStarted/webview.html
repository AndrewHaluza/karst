How Karst works

    Report an issue

    Use this when Karst itself fails, for example a stuck stage or a blank panel. You review the redacted report before it leaves your machine.

    What the report contains

    Karst collects its own diagnostics (runtime versions, stage and gate state, effective configuration, bounded logs) and redacts secrets, paths and credentials. Approve the report and Karst hands the redacted summary to a prefilled GitHub issue in your browser. You still press Submit there. Ticket descriptions and other prompt text are left out unless you opt in during the review step. For work items in your own project, create a ticket instead.
