    How Karst works

      Report an issue

      Click this when Karst itself behaves unexpectedly — a ticket stuck at a stage, a gate that will not run, a panel that goes blank. Karst collects its own diagnostics (runtime versions, stage and gate state, effective configuration, bounded logs), redacts secrets, paths and credentials, then opens the report for you to read before anything leaves your machine. Approve it and Karst hands the redacted summary to a prefilled GitHub issue in your browser; you still press Submit there.

      Ticket descriptions and other prompt text are left out unless you opt in during the review step. For work items in your own project, create a ticket instead.
